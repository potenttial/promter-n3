Mexico's tourist fees in USD and CAD: DNR and Visitax explained

The non-resident fee rises to 1,158 pesos in 2027 (about US$63 / C$91), usually in your ticket. Quintana Roo's Visitax is 293 pesos (about US$16 / C$23), paid online.

By Travelytix Editors · November 25, 2026 · 1 min read

Two charges apply to most foreign visitors to the Mexican Caribbean. Here they are in your currency.

The fees

Fee Pesos US$ C$ How you pay
Non-resident fee (DNR), 2026 983 ~53 ~78 Usually in your airline ticket
Non-resident fee (DNR), 2027 1,158 ~63 ~91 Usually in your airline ticket
Visitax (Quintana Roo) 293 ~16 ~23 Official state portal

Conversions at 18.42 pesos per US$ (Banxico, Nov. 17) and 12.68 pesos per C$ (Nov. 25); amounts rounded.

Tips

  • Check your ticket breakdown for the DNR before paying it again anywhere.
  • Visitax: use only the official Quintana Roo portal. Hoteliers reported airport payment demands more than 80% above the official fee, and the state plans to move collection fully online.
  • Keep the QR receipt on your phone.

Couple’s total for 2027

About US$158 or C$229 for two people, DNR plus Visitax.

FAQ

How much is Mexico's tourist entry fee in 2027?

1,158 pesos, about US$63 or C$91, usually included in your airline ticket.

How much is the Visitax?

293 pesos, about US$16 or C$23, paid on the official Quintana Roo portal.

Do I pay the Visitax at the airport?

The state is moving collection online; pay only on the official portal.
