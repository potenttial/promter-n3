Spending Thanksgiving in Cancún? Here's what to expect this week

Cancún is the top international Thanksgiving pick for Americans. Hotels ran about 73% full last week, sargassum is low and hurricane season ends Sunday. A quick checklist.

By Travelytix Editors · November 25, 2026 · 1 min read

Cancún is the most popular international destination for Americans over Thanksgiving, according to Caribbean Journal. If you’re there this week, here’s the picture.

Conditions this week

  • Crowds: Cancún hotels were 73.1% full the week of Nov. 14–20; expect busier days over the holiday weekend.
  • Sargassum: low. On Nov. 11, 96 of 140 monitored beaches in Quintana Roo were at the lowest level.
  • Weather: Atlantic hurricane season officially ends Nov. 30.

Quick checklist

  1. Passport valid for your whole stay; e-gates need an e- passport valid 180+ days.
  2. Digital FMM: if you use an e-gate, scan the QR code on your ticket and keep the form until you leave.
  3. Visitax: pay the state visitor fee only on the official portal, 293 pesos (about US$16).
  4. Transport: use authorized airport taxis or pre-booked shuttles.
  5. Thanksgiving dinner: many resorts host a holiday menu; book your table early.

Getting around

The free Nichupté bridge, opened in May 2026, links downtown Cancún with the hotel zone across the lagoon.
