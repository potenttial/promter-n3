Mexico entry requirements for U.S. and Canadian travelers (2026)

Passport, no visa for tourism up to 180 days, e-gates for e-passport holders and a digital FMM. What Americans and Canadians need to enter Mexico by air.

By Travelytix Editors · November 25, 2026 · 1 min read

Entering Mexico by air is straightforward for U.S. and Canadian citizens. Here’s what you need, based on official Mexican and Canadian government guidance.

The essentials

  • Passport: a valid passport for your entire stay. To use the automated e-gates, it must be an e-passport valid for at least 180 days.
  • Visa: not required for tourism. Canada’s government notes stays of up to 180 days, with the length set by the immigration officer.
  • Entry fee: the non-resident fee is usually included in your airline ticket.
  • FMM: you get either a passport stamp or, at an e-gate, a ticket with a QR code to download your digital FMM. Keep it until you leave.

E-gates at Cancún

Open to travelers 18 or older, traveling for tourism and not accompanied by minors, with passports from 30 countries including the U.S. and Canada. Families with kids use the regular booths.

Before you fly

  • Check your passport’s expiration date.
  • Save your hotel address and return flight details.
  • Canadians: Canada’s government says to confirm current requirements with the Embassy of Mexico before travel.

Rules can change; always check official sources close to departure.

FAQ

Do Americans need a visa for Mexico?

No, not for tourism. A valid passport is required.

Can I use the e-gates at Cancún airport?

Yes, if you're 18+, traveling for tourism without minors, with a U.S. or Canadian e-passport valid for 180+ days.

What is the FMM?

Mexico's multiple migration form. At e-gates you download it as a digital form via a QR code and keep it until departure.
