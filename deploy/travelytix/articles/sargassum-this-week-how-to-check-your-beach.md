# Sargassum this week: how to check your beach before you go

> 96 of 140 monitored beaches in Quintana Roo were at the low level on Nov. 11. Three free tools to check sargassum before booking or heading to the beach.

Travelytix Editors · 2026-11-26 · Travelytix

Late fall and winter are the low season for sargassum in the Mexican Caribbean. On Nov. 11, 96 of 140 monitored beaches in Quintana Roo were at the lowest level.

## Three ways to check
1. Quintana Roo's sargassum traffic light: beach-by-beach levels from the state.
2. NOAA's Sargassum Inundation Risk tool: daily risk maps at about 1 km resolution since 2026.
3. UNAM's satellite viewer (LANOT): shows mats at sea and where they're heading.

## When it's usually worst

Roughly April to September. Winter dates are the safer bet.

## If you see sargassum
- Isla Mujeres' Playa Norte and Cozumel's west coast are often calmer.
- Many resorts clean early in the morning.
- Plan a cenote day; inland waters aren't affected.
