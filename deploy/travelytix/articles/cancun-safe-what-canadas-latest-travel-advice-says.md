# Is Cancún safe? What Canada's latest travel advice says and how to stay smart

> Canada's government advises a high degree of caution for Mexico overall; Quintana Roo is not on its avoid-travel list. Practical tips from the official advice, updated Nov. 18, 2026.

Travelytix Editors · 2026-11-26 · Travelytix

Safety questions top the list for North American travelers. Here's what Canada's official travel advice says, updated Nov. 18, 2026.

## The level
- Mexico overall: "Exercise a high degree of caution."
- Quintana Roo is not on Canada's list of regions to avoid non-essential travel.

## What the advice flags for resort areas
- Violent crime still occurs, including gang fights in hotels, nightclubs and restaurants.
- Petty crime, armed robbery and assaults, sometimes involving people posing as police or taxi drivers.
- Drink spiking and overcharging at bars.
- Strong riptides; beaches are often unsupervised and without flags.

## Smart moves
1. Use reputable taxis or ride-hailing apps; don't hail cabs on the street.
2. Watch your drink and avoid late-night isolated areas.
3. Swim where there are lifeguards and respect red flags.
4. Save 911 (emergencies) and the consular agencies: Cancún +52 55-5724-9795; Playa del Carmen +52 55-5724-9796.

U.S. travelers should check the State Department's Mexico advisory at travel.state.gov before departure.
