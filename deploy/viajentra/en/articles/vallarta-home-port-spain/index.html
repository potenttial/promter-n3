Vallarta will be Mexico's first cruise home port: what to know before you book

Mexico will make Puerto Vallarta a cruise home port where voyages start and end. What changes versus a port of call, what's confirmed and what isn't.

By Viajentra Newsroom · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read

Straight to the point: Mexico announced that Puerto Vallarta will be the country’s first cruise home port, so voyages will start and end there rather than stopping for a few hours.

Port of call Home port
Time ashore A few hours Days before and after
How you arrive By ship By plane, then you board
Hotel Usually not Yes, one or more nights

Confirmed: three piers, about 95,172 m² of land and water, a priority in Vallarta’s 2026–2031 port plan.

Not confirmed: opening date, investment or cruise lines. Don’t book anything based on this terminal yet.

From January to July 2026, Mexican ports handled 7.5 million cruise passengers (+16.2%) and US$648.9 million in spending.

Source: Real Estate Market & Lifestyle, 23/09/2026
