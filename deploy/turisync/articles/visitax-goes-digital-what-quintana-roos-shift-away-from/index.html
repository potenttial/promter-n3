Visitax goes digital: what Quintana Roo's shift away from airport collection means for travel platforms

Quintana Roo plans to end in-person collection of its 293-peso visitor fee at Cancún airport. Hoteliers report demands more than 80% above the fee. The integration opportunity for OTAs and airlines.

by Turisync Analysis · November 25, 2026 · 1 min read

Quintana Roo’s Visitax, a 293-peso contribution paid by foreign visitors, is moving online. Governor Mara Lezama said physical collection at Cancún airport should give way to a digital system, PAX News reported on Nov. 13, citing El Economista.

Why the change

  • Overcharging: David Ortiz Mena, president of the Mexican Caribbean Hotel Council, reported airport payment demands more than 80% above the official fee.
  • Reputation: he warned that unexpected charges damage the destination’s image.
  • Friction: the tour-operator association Acotur said approaching travelers near departure can feel invasive, while noting it had no formal member complaints.

How it works today

Payment is made on the official state Visitax portal. Travelers can retrieve a receipt with their passport number and download a QR code. No implementation date for ending airport collection has been announced.

The opportunity for platforms

Player Possible integration
OTAs and tour operators Prompt or bundle the official payment at checkout
Airlines Reminder in pre-trip emails and apps
Hotels Check at booking confirmation, not at the front desk

The lesson travel tech keeps relearning applies here: a fee is a payments problem and a trust problem at once. Moving it to the booking flow solves both.

Key data

  • Visitax: 293 pesos per foreign visitor (about US$16 at 18.42 pesos per dollar).
  • Reported airport overcharges: more than 80% above the fee.

What is the Visitax?

Is Visitax still collected at Cancún airport?

Quintana Roo plans to end in-person airport collection and move fully online; no date had been set as of Nov. 13, 2026.
