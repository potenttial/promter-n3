# Cancún airport's biometric e-gates: 40 planned, 30 eligible countries and a digital FMM

> Cancún is scaling automated immigration from 26 to 40 e-gates and from 48 to 54 manual booths. Who can use them and why it matters for peak-season throughput.

Turisync Analysis · 2026-11-26 · Turisync

Mexico's busiest leisure airport is betting on biometrics to absorb peak-season crowds.

## The rollout

|  | Before | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal 4 e-gates | 10 operating | 20 |
| Terminal 3 e-gates | 16 operating | 20 (new system) |
| Manual immigration booths | 48 | 54 |

Figures announced by Governor Mara Lezama with the National Migration Institute (INM) in April 2026, per Riviera Maya News.

## Who can use them

According to Mexico's foreign ministry, automated migration filters are open to travelers who:
- Are 18 or older and not traveling with minors.
- Enter for tourism.
- Hold an ordinary e-passport valid for at least 180 days.
- Hold a passport from one of 30 countries, including the United States, Canada, the U.K., Spain, Germany, Japan and South Korea.

After the gate prints a ticket, travelers scan a QR code to download the digital FMM (multiple migration form) and keep it until departure.

## Network effect

The same filters run in Mexico City (T1 and T2), San José del Cabo (T2), Guadalajara, Puerto Vallarta, Querétaro and Tijuana. For airlines and tour operators, faster processing shortens the gap between landing and hotel check-in, a pain point in winter peaks.

## What's missing

There is no public data yet on average processing times before and after the rollout. That is the metric the industry should ask for.

## FAQ

**How many e-gates does Cancún airport have?**
26 were operating in April 2026 (10 in Terminal 4, 16 in Terminal 3), with a target of 40.

**Can U.S. and Canadian travelers use the e-gates?**
Yes, if they are 18 or older, travel for tourism without minors and hold an e-passport valid for at least 180 days.

**What is the digital FMM?**
The electronic multiple migration form. Travelers download it by scanning a QR code on the e-gate ticket and keep it until they leave Mexico.
