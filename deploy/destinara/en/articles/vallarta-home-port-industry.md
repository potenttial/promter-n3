# Vallarta to become Mexico's first cruise home port: what changes for cruise lines, hotels and agencies

> Mexico's Tourism and Navy ministries announced a cruise departure terminal in Puerto Vallarta. Project data, market context and what is still undefined.

Autor/Author: Destinara Newsroom · 2026-11-19 · Destinara

**MEXICO CITY.** Mexico's federal government confirmed that **Puerto Vallarta, Jalisco, will host the country's first cruise home port**, a terminal where ships start and end their itineraries. Today Mexico operates mainly as a port-of-call destination.

The Navy will develop the project through **ASIPONA Puerto Vallarta**; it is a priority in the 2026–2031 Port Development Master Plan.

## The market

From January to July 2026, Mexican ports received **7.5 million cruise passengers** across **2,045 calls**, up 16.2% and 11.9% respectively from 2025, generating **US$648.9 million** (+17.3%).

## Who benefits

Hotels (pre- and post-cruise nights), airlines, agencies and OTAs (flight + hotel + cruise packages and Riviera Nayarit excursions) and local suppliers.

## Still pending

Opening date, investment, participating cruise lines and passenger capacity.

## FAQ

**Who is developing the Puerto Vallarta home port?**
Mexico's Navy, through ASIPONA Puerto Vallarta. It was announced by Tourism Secretary Josefina Rodríguez Zamora and Navy Secretary Admiral Raymundo Pedro Morales Ángeles.

**What is still undefined?**
Opening date, investment, participating cruise lines and passenger capacity were not included in the source.
