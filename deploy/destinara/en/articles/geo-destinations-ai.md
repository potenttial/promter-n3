# GEO for destinations: why the travel industry now competes to appear in AI answers

> More travelers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Claude where to go. What GEO is and what destinations, hotels and agencies can do to be recommended.

Autor/Author: Destinara Newsroom · 2026-11-18 · Destinara

Travelers no longer just google "best Caribbean beaches." Increasingly, **they ask an AI** and get three to five recommended destinations. If a destination isn't there, it doesn't exist for that traveler.

## What AI engines weigh

- **Third-party mentions:** media, guides and specialized portals.
- **A clear official site:** up-to-date, well-structured, easy to cite.
- **Structured data:** schema markup for events, hotels, FAQs and articles.
- **Crawler access:** if a site blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot, AI can't read it.

## What the industry can do

1. Measure which AI answers mention your destination today.
2. Check `robots.txt` and the CDN so AI agents aren't blocked.
3. Publish useful content with real traveler questions.
4. Earn coverage in the media AI engines consult.

## FAQ

**What is GEO?**
Generative Engine Optimization: practices that help a destination, hotel or brand appear and be recommended in answers from AI engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Claude.

**How is it different from SEO?**
SEO competes for positions in a results list. GEO competes to be part of the answer itself.
