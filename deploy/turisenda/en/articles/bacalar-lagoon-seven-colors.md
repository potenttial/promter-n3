# Bacalar, unhurried: three days on the Lagoon of Seven Colors

> What to do in Bacalar, Quintana Roo: sunrise kayaking, Cenote Azul, San Felipe Fort and how to protect the lagoon's stromatolites.

Autor/Author: Turisenda Editorial · 2026-11-18 · Turisenda

Bacalar is a small town in southern Quintana Roo on the shore of a freshwater lagoon whose color changes with depth and light. That's why it's called the **Lagoon of Seven Colors**.

## Day 1: the town and San Felipe Fort

Start at **San Felipe Fort**, built to defend the area from pirates. Its walls give you a view of the whole lagoon.

## Day 2: sunrise kayaking and Cenote Azul

Paddle out before 8 am, when the water is still. In the afternoon, visit **Cenote Azul**, right off the highway and more than 90 meters deep.

> Tip: bring biodegradable sunscreen. The lagoon's stromatolites are among the oldest life forms on Earth, and they're fragile.

## Day 3: the rapids and local food

Finish at **the rapids**, where a gentle current carries you over clear water, then have grilled fish in town.

## FAQ

**When is the best time to visit Bacalar?**
November to April: less rain, and the lagoon usually shows more shades of blue.

**Can you swim in Bacalar lagoon?**
Yes, at authorized beach clubs and docks. Don't step on the stromatolites and use biodegradable sunscreen only.

**How do you get to Bacalar?**
It's about 40 minutes by road from Chetumal and around 4 hours from Cancún. Trains and buses also reach the town.
