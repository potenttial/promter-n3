# San Diego to Valle de Guadalupe in a weekend

> Driving from San Diego to Baja California's Wine Route: border crossing, timing, Ensenada stops and what to know about car insurance.

Autor/Author: Turisenda Editorial · 2026-11-11 · Turisenda

**Valle de Guadalupe** produces most of Mexico's wine and sits about two hours by car from San Diego, depending on the border wait.

## The route

Cross at Tijuana and take the scenic highway to Ensenada along the Pacific. From Ensenada, the valley is about 30 minutes inland.

## What to plan for

Bring your passport, buy **Mexican car insurance** before crossing and time your return outside peak border hours.

## FAQ

**Do I need Mexican insurance to drive from the US?**
Yes. Most US policies don't cover you in Mexico, so buy a Mexican policy before you cross.
