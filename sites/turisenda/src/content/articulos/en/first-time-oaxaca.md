Oaxaca City is best explored on foot. The historic center is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and most essentials are a few blocks from the zócalo.

## Day 1: Santo Domingo and the markets

Visit **Santo Domingo de Guzmán** church and its ethnobotanical garden. Then walk to **20 de Noviembre Market** and its smoky grill aisle, where you pick your meat and they cook it in front of you.

## Day 2: Monte Albán and mezcal

Head early to **Monte Albán**, the ancient Zapotec capital, about 20 minutes from the city. In the afternoon, taste mezcal downtown and ask about the agave behind each one.
